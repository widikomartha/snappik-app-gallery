## Delivered behavior

The isolated JSON `POST /webhooks/midtrans` route validates request size/shape, Midtrans's SHA-512 signature and the persisted sandbox merchant/order, IDR gross amount/currency and QRIS channel. It accepts no browser payment claim. Invalid/unmapped notifications cannot create grants. Valid notifications commit a minimized deterministic receipt digest and a job containing only its event UUID on the `payments` PostgreSQL database queue, atomically, before returning 200. Equivalent notifications deduplicate. Receipt/outbox persistence failure returns 503 for provider retry; raw signatures, provider payloads, customer details and credentials are not stored in history or queued payloads.

The worker retrieves authoritative sandbox status through the same adapter used by checkout recovery and reconciliation. It checks merchant/order identity, exact whole-IDR gross amount, currency, QRIS, transaction identity and result state. QRIS settlement additionally requires success result code, accepted fraud status when supplied and a valid nonfuture `settlement_time` no earlier than order creation. Time is interpreted at second precision in GMT+7 and persisted as UTC. A callback or return URL alone never establishes payment.

`ApplyPayment` is the only financial writer. It locks persisted user, account access, order, voucher root/reservation and receipt, then applies all effects in one transaction: a unique immutable grant, access projection extension, unique immutable voucher redemption, reservation consumption and paid-order timestamps/state. The extension is exactly 720 hours from the later of authoritative payment time and current access end, including remaining trial time. Different orders serialize on the account; duplicate receipts/orders apply once. Suspension remains effective even when a previously purchased contract settles. Renewals preserve project metadata, acknowledged choices, confirmations and module deadlines.

Verified deny/cancel/expire/failure releases a held reservation. Core 404, transport uncertainty, browser abandonment and local expiry retain capacity. A confirmed terminal order cannot reopen after stale pending/missing observations; contradictions are flagged. Authoritative late settlement honors the original price, discount/version and duration even after voucher deactivation/expiry or release/resale of capacity, granting once and recording `late_settlement_after_release` for investigation. It never recomputes availability to deny an already paid contract.

Paid orders cannot regress on old pending/failure observations. Refund/partial-refund/chargeback/unknown states and identity/amount/time mismatches produce durable review records and safe warning alerts. They do not create another grant, restore voucher usage, execute refunds or claw back access. Paid state and immutable ledger remain visible during review. Flags are sticky for operator investigation; TASK-012 owns the full operations UI.

Checkout creation/recovery and payment processing share one per-order PostgreSQL session advisory lock, preventing creation/status recovery from overwriting a concurrent payment. Payment receipts retain attempts, due/enqueue/start/finish times, safe errors and verified result digests. Transient status errors back off for at most five attempts per receipt; exhausted/nonretryable failures require review. Worker errors retain only safe diagnostics. The scheduler queues never-checked orders after two minutes, active orders after five minutes and paid/failed orders daily, restoring overdue/interrupted receipts after five minutes while suppressing continuous duplicate outbox creation. Flagged orders support explicit provider recheck.

The trusted operator command `snappik:payments-reconcile --order=<application-order-uuid>` and owner-only `POST /admin/orders/{order}/recheck` queue an authoritative status fetch. The owner endpoint rechecks persisted admin permission, records its actor in payment history and refuses payment status/amount input. There is no manual mark-paid or free entitlement endpoint. The scheduled command reports the number of flagged orders. Billing/order views now show verified paid, provider-confirmed failure and review truthfully, including paid orders under review, and remove payment-opening controls after terminal outcomes.
